# BRIEF — THE LONG COLD

- **Topic / logline:** A sun-dimming volcanic winter drops the city to -22°C overnight. You walk ~900 miles south through a frozen highway of abandoned cars toward the last warm coast, trading warmth like currency, and survive because you never stop.
- **Premise class:** Natural disaster / survival (global freeze). Zombie premise explicitly declined.
- **Target runtime:** 10 minutes → 1876 words @ ~3.1 wps = 10:05
- **Voice + pace:** `en-US-AndrewNeural`, `--rate=+10%`, Edge-TTS (Kaggle only)
- **POV:** Second person, present tense ("you")
- **Style / look:** Ink-and-watercolour cartoon on warm parchment/paper grain, as per `refs/ref_style_01` and `refs/ref_style_02` — ARTSTYLE ONLY, never a layout template. Rounded white face, dot eyes, dark simple body; loose ink outlines, wet-on-wet washes, limited palette (slate blue-grey cold, amber/ochre firelight), visible brush texture, subtle paper border.
- **Hard rules — MUST appear:** frost on the *inside* of a window; an open car door in a dead traffic jam; a fire in a metal bucket; breath vapour in every exterior beat; the sun as a power-less pale coin.
- **Hard rules — MUST NEVER appear:** readable text/logos/signage (gibberish text is a QC fail), gore or graphic bodies (the gas-station man is implied, never detailed), crossfades/dissolves/fades in the edit, on-screen captions, third-person pronouns for the protagonist.
- **Captions:** none
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (1920x1080)
- **Platform:** YouTube long-form
- **Audio boundary:** NO TTS, audio mixing, or FFmpeg in this workspace. Frames + scripts here; encode on Kaggle.
