The train stopped between stations, and the lights went out. Then came the sound no one could place: water, moving fast through the tunnel toward them. Priya pressed the emergency intercom. No answer. Behind her, a man tried to force the doors. “Stop,” she said. “We don't know what's outside.” A child asked if they were going to drown. Priya looked at the water rising beneath the tracks, then at the maintenance light blinking in the distance. She had one route map, thirty strangers, and no way to call for help.
