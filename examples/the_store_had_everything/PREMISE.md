## Core premise

An ordinary **night-shift stock clerk** and a **delivery driver** become trapped inside a large outdoor-supply superstore during a sudden infected outbreak.

The store contains food, bottled water, radios, batteries, first-aid supplies, ropes, lights, filters and camping equipment. It appears to have everything needed to survive, but its automated public-address system continues cheerfully announcing sales. The repeated announcements attract infected toward the glass storefront.

The clerk knows the building layout, shutters and inventory. The driver has keys to a van in the loading bay. Neither is a survival instructor or an all-knowing expert, and their decisions must emerge through action rather than lessons.

## Immediate objective

Cross the darkened store, reach the locked security office and shut down the public-address system before the storefront fails. Then decide whether to remain among abundant supplies or risk reaching the delivery van.
