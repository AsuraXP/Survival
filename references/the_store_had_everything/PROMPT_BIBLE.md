# The Store Had Everything — Prompt Bible

Status: revised after the user's background-cohesion and Nolan-hairstyle corrections. Proposed for corrected-reference-pack approval.

## Reference hierarchy

Use references together, not as substitutes for one another:

1. **Scene/location master:** controls camera, architecture, landmark placement, lighting and existing crowd state.
2. **Nolan and/or Lena identity reference:** controls character construction, exact hair silhouette and wardrobe.
3. **Previous approved frame:** controls the immediate physical action when the shot is a direct continuation.
4. **User style source:** controls general simple character construction only; it does not control Nolan's haircut, wardrobe, composition or background.
5. **Original S01 hybrid:** anchors the original C01/C02 character appearance, including Nolan's spiky fringe; its realistic background is not active.
6. **S02 unified cartoon:** anchors shared outlines, cel shading and vivid colour relationships; its portrait crop is not a video frame.

Never prompt a same-scene continuation from text alone. Never mix style-test backgrounds with the clean opening background plate as though they were identical.

## Canonical character prompts

### Nolan — C01

Nolan Brooks, 26, lean, approximately 3.5 heads tall in the selected simple cartoon construction. Oversized round pale head, clean dark hand-drawn outline, **full dark-brown side-swept spiky fringe with pointed locks and lively upward tufts matching the original C01/S01 silhouette**, solid-black vertical oval eyes, simple curved brows and mouth, no nose detail, thin neck and limbs, simplified hands and shoes. Vivid teal fleece vest over a charcoal dark long-sleeve shirt, dark work trousers, dark sneakers with pale soles, plain black lanyard with blank light-grey badge. Worried determination expressed through brow angle, mouth, head tilt and posture. No backpack or radio in the opening.

Nolan's hair must never become flat, neatly combed down, a thin side-parted cap, or the three-stroke bald hairstyle in the general construction source. Those are rejected identity errors.

### Lena — C02

Lena Ortiz, 34, ordinary capable build, approximately 3.5 heads tall in the same simple cartoon construction. Oversized round pale head, clean dark outline, solid-black vertical oval eyes, simple curved brows and mouth, no nose detail, thin neck and limbs. Smooth black hair tied back with one narrow braid over her anatomical right shoulder when visible. Warm amber delivery jacket over dark burgundy shirt, navy cargo trousers, dark work shoes. Van keys canonically held in her anatomical right hand during the opening; right hand means the viewer-left hand in a straight-on front view. Worried focus through brow angle, mouth and body posture. No backpack or radio in the opening paragraph.

## Canonical world prompt

Unified vivid hand-drawn 2D cel-cartoon environment, related dark outlines around architecture and props, simplified geometric outdoor equipment, large clean colour shapes, two-or-three-tone cel shading, restrained stylized shadows, saturated teal, amber, orange and electric-blue accents, deep-blue night beyond the storefront, warm amber practical lights, cyan security-office monitor glow, broad graphic floor-light bands, recognizable props and atmospheric depth without realistic micro-texture. Characters remain simple while receiving the same cel shading, contact shadows and lighting logic so they occupy the same world naturally.

## Required story-frame prompt order

1. Narration moment and emotional purpose.
2. `scene_id`, `setup_id`, exact master and previous approved frame.
3. Fixed character positions, route direction, head/body attention, props and hand state.
4. Fixed landmark, infected, shutter, equipment and lighting state.
5. The one intended camera/expression/action change.
6. Relevant canonical character and world descriptions, including Nolan's exact spiky hair silhouette.
7. Preservation constraints and negative constraints.

## Global negative constraints

No realistic protagonist faces, detailed irises, detailed noses, complex facial anatomy, comic-book muscles or changed head/body proportions. No flat/combed-down Nolan hair, three-stroke Nolan hair, extra fingers or props, swapped key hand, missing or switched braid, changed wardrobe, backpacks or radio before their story acquisition. No moved shelves, tents, coolers, kayaks, stairs or office; no extra interior people or exits; no uncovered glass after the shutter closes; no gore, captions, readable brand text, logos, borders or watermarks.

Do not compensate for a weak result by redesigning the location or changing a character's physical position. Reject and correct the failed variable.
