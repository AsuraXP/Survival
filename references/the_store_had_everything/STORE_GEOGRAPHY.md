# The Store Had Everything — Opening Store Geography

Status: proposed canonical geography for reference-pack approval. `store_floor_plan.svg` is the schematic companion. These measurements define a fictional store; they are not a real building plan.

## Coordinate system

- Origin `(0,0)` is the inside front-left corner of the sales floor.
- `x` increases toward the right/east wall; `y` increases toward the rear.
- Sales-floor footprint: 80 m wide × 45 m deep.
- Stockroom/loading zone continues behind the sales floor to `y=60`.
- One storey for the sales floor; the security office is elevated above the rear-right area and reached by one fixed staircase.

## Opening route

`C03 → along the checkout line → camping entrance → past T01 → hide behind B01 → around K01-side displays → S01 → O01`

The route is not a teleport. Each story frame must identify the current segment and preserve the direction of travel.

## Opening camera setup A01

- Camera is inside/near checkout three, looking diagonally toward the rear-right office.
- Front glass and blue night occupy the left of frame.
- Checkout equipment occupies the left foreground.
- Orange tent and blue coolers sit in the central depth.
- Green kayaks remain on the right wall.
- Security office glows cyan at rear-right above the stairs.
- `opening_store_background.png` is the visual master for this setup once approved.

## Opening character blocking

At the first narration beat:

- Nolan is at checkout three, ahead of Lena on the route toward the office. His left shoulder is toward the storefront; his body is ready to move toward the rear.
- Lena is one step behind and to Nolan's route-right. Her van keys are canonically in her **anatomical right hand**. Her braid remains over her right shoulder when visible from the front/three-quarter view.
- Neither has a backpack or radio in the opening paragraph.
- They may turn their heads toward the glass, each other, or the office only when the shot's narration supports that attention change. Their feet do not move until the planned crossing beat.

## Required later state changes

- Lena acquires the hand-crank radio only at the camping display in narration paragraph 4.
- The keys drop and are retrieved in paragraph 5; they cannot simply vanish.
- The front shutter changes from raised to lowered only during paragraph 9.
- The shutter's inward deformation appears only after the bang in paragraph 13.
