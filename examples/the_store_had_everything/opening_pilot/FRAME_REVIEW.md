# Opening Pilot — Continuing Frame Review Log

Review owner: assistant, delegated by the user on 2026-10-24. The user still receives the assembled pilot and can request changes; production no longer waits for manual clearance of every individual batch.

## Required review for every generated frame

1. Narration match and intended emotion.
2. Native dimensions and unified cel-cartoon rendering.
3. C01/C02 identity, including Nolan's spiky hair and Lena's braid/key hand.
4. Anatomy, expression, pose and readability.
5. Character position and movement relative to the previous accepted frame.
6. Fixed landmarks, camera direction, crowd/glass/shutter state and lighting.
7. Props and story acquisition state; no early radio or backpacks.
8. Unwanted text, logos, borders, extra people, gore or unsupported future events.
9. Adjacent-frame sequence continuity before the image becomes a reference.

Only `approved_user` or `accepted_internal_qa` frames may guide direct continuations. Rejected frames are moved to `rejected/` and never used as production references.
